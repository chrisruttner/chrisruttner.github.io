Privacy Policy

OpenCode Supervisor V1 is a private, personal-use application restricted to its owner and explicitly authorized accounts.

Google user data

The application may access Google Drive files and folders that the authorized account can access and that are required for an owner-requested task.

Use of data

Google user data is used only to perform owner-authorized automation, validate task state, and safely complete or recover requested file operations.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared for unrelated purposes. Data may be processed by service providers only when necessary to carry out an owner-requested operation.

Storage and retention

Project data remains in the owner's configured storage locations. Temporary or recovery data may be retained only as needed to complete, verify, or safely recover an authorized operation.

Security and access

Access is restricted to authorized accounts and configured resources. Credentials and authentication tokens are not published on this website.

Revocation

Access can be revoked at any time through the authorized Google account or by removing the application's access to the relevant files and folders.
