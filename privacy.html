Privacy Policy

OpenCode Supervisor V1

OpenCode Supervisor V1 is a private, personal-use automation service operated for its owner and explicitly authorized accounts. This policy describes how data is handled when the service uses Google Drive and related infrastructure.

Data accessed

The service may access files and folders in Google Drive that have been explicitly shared with or approved for the dedicated Supervisor account. It may also process task instructions, project files, generated changes, runtime state, recovery metadata, and execution results required to perform an authorized task.

How data is used

Data is used only to execute user-requested tasks, validate project state, enforce safety and writeback controls, maintain recovery state, and return task status or results. Data is not sold, rented, or used for advertising.

Third-party processing

Depending on the configured task, data may be processed by infrastructure and service providers required to operate the workflow, including Google Drive, GitHub Actions, Cloudflare Workers, Telegram, and AI providers explicitly enabled by the owner. Provider use is restricted by the Supervisor's configured privacy and cost policies.

Storage and retention

Google Drive project files remain in Google Drive. Temporary execution workspaces may be created during a task. Private recovery records may be retained in a dedicated Google Drive runtime location so interrupted write operations can be reconciled safely. Deployment credentials and OAuth tokens are intended to be stored only in private secret/configuration stores, not in this public website.

Security

The service is designed to restrict execution to approved accounts, approved Google Drive locations, explicit task modes, and controlled provider/runtime settings. No public user registration is provided.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the application's Google account access through Google Account security settings or remove the dedicated Supervisor account's access to shared Drive folders.
